💳 Suica IC Card — Your Main Travel Card

No JR Pass on this trip. Load ¥10,000 at Narita Airport on arrival. The Suica card covers all inter-city and city rail, metro lines, city buses, and convenience stores nationwide. Top up at any station or 7-Eleven ATM.

Muslim Pro app

Certified halal restaurant finder for Japan. Always verify halal status on arrival — certification can change.

Cash — carry ¥5,000–10,000 at all times

Power bank — 10,000mAh or more
15,000–25,000 steps per day will drain your phone. Essential for DisneySea and Miyajima full days.
✓
Comfortable walking shoes — broken in before Day 1
Do not try new shoes in Japan. Break them in at home several weeks before departure.
